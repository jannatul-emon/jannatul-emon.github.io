The problem

Good tradespeople lose calls to businesses that are easier to find.

When a pipe bursts, people don't scroll. They call one of the first three businesses on the map. If you're not there, the job goes to someone else.

Without local SEO

  • ✕Your profile sits on page two of Maps.
  • ✕Old directory listings show the wrong phone number.
  • ✕Few reviews, and none of them answered.
  • ✕One generic "Services" page for every job and suburb.
  • ✕No idea which marketing brings in calls.

With a local SEO plan

  • ✓You show in the top 3 for the jobs you want.
  • ✓Same name, address and phone on every listing.
  • ✓A steady flow of new reviews, every one answered.
  • ✓A page for each service and each area you cover.
  • ✓A monthly report on calls, rankings and leads.
